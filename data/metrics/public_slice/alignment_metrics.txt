VERDICT-INFERENCE SCOPE (read first)
- Verdict accuracy and the TP/TN/FP/FN counts below reflect our extraction pipeline's interpretation of each system's review. Native verdicts are produced by both System A configurations (Opus and GPT-4o), which emit an explicit Decision field; for the other four configurations the verdict is inferred from review text.
- The values below are single run (run 1) on the 9-paper Named Papers slice. The website homepage displays 3-run means and will not numerically match.
- The paper appendix's sensitivity audit (covering a separate 48-paper safety/alignment benchmark) shows verdict-level numbers vary with inference method, while concern-level metrics — strict/loose recall, phantom rate, decisive recall — do not.
- The audit has since been extended to the 9-paper public slice this file describes. Per-configuration flip rate between pipeline and final (audited) verdict: A.GPT4o 0/9, A.Opus 0/9, L.GPT4o 2/9, L.Opus 0/9, M.GPT4o 7/9 (all UNRELIABLE), O.Opus 1/9. The reconciled CSV is at data/audit/verdict_inference_named_9.csv.
- System M reviews additionally contain multi-agent coordination artifacts that make verdict inference unreliable for that configuration regardless of method.
